About

Music has been my primary language since I was three years old. A multi-instrumentalist with a foundation in classical and jazz saxophone, I have spent my life exploring the textures of piano, drums, trombone, and the deeper voices of the tenor and baritone sax.

I write cinematic Lo-Fi Jazz — instrumental, atmospheric, always in motion. Each piece begins somewhere specific: a room, a night, a light on water. The recording keeps the room in it.
